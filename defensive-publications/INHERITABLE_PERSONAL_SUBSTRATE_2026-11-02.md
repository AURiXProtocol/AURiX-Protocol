## Posthumous persistence of an operator-declared record, with living-operator partition, per-heir bounded views, provenance-attested surfacing, and a structural bar against posthumous invention

## SUMMARY

Most systems forget a person twice: once at the end of each session, and once at death. Digital-legacy and "griefbot" products answer the second forgetting by simulation: they generate speech the dead person never spoke. This disclosure describes the opposite. A person's own declared record persists after death, split while they are alive into what passes to which heirs, what stays sealed and for how long, and what is destroyed. Heirs can explore and question it, but every item shown carries a receipt back to the person's own recorded words, and the system is structurally barred from inventing anything beyond the record. Where the record is silent, the system says so.

Terms used below: **navigable space**, a visual map of a person's record that a viewer can move through, organized by the person's own declared relations and emphasis. **"Flyable" / "throwable"**, ways of moving through that map and sending a bounded portion of it to someone else.

## FIELD OF THE INVENTION

The invention relates to computer-implemented systems for the persistence, custody, inheritance, and posthumous navigation of an operator-declared personal substrate; more particularly to consent partitions fixed during the operator's life, per-heir bounded views, provenance attestation of posthumously surfaced material, and structural constraints preventing a deceased person's record from being extended, simulated, or invented beyond what they declared.

---

## DEFINITIONS

**Substrate.** The operator-declared personal record: writings, recordings, declared relations, declared weights, and the navigable space assembled from them, as described below.

**Inheritability partition.** The operator's living declaration assigning each region of the substrate to exactly one disposition: inheritable to a named heir class, sealed for a declared term, or extinguished at death. Undeclared material defaults to sealed.

**Heir class.** A receiver class — for example family, friends, helpers, public, named individuals — fixed by the operator while living, to which a bounded view of inheritable material is granted after death.

**Bounded view.** A rendering of the substrate restricted to one heir class's granted material, with all other material absent rather than hidden, such that the view's completeness claim is itself attestable.

**Provenance receipt.** Per-item attestation that a surfaced item traces to the operator's own verbatim record at a cited location.

**Register-preserving interrogation.** Answering an heir's question exclusively by retrieval and arrangement of the operator's own recorded words, preserving the operator's diction, such that every sentence in an answer carries a receipt.

**Posthumous-invention bar.** The structural constraint that the system may never synthesize, extrapolate, or simulate content attributed to the deceased operator beyond the declared record: no invented opinion, no generated speech in the operator's voice, no answer where the record is silent. Where the record is silent, the system says the record is silent.

**Custody transfer.** The attested transition of the substrate's keys and stewardship at death, executed per the operator's living declaration, recorded append-only.

**Protected-custody mode.** An institutional deployment for records of persons who cannot declare — the disappeared, the unidentified, the deceased without partition — in which the partition authority is a declared legal proxy and every constraint of the invention binds the institution rather than serving it.

## THE INVENTION

The invention comprises the following constitutive elements arranged in a specified structural relationship.

**Element 1 — Living partition, operator-only.** Only the living operator assigns inheritability. The partition is declared region by region, revisable at will during life, recorded append-only, and immutable at death. The system may propose nothing into the inheritable set; undeclared material seals. Consent is therefore the inheritance mechanism itself, not a setting upon it.

**Element 2 — Persistence beyond the operator.** At death the substrate persists as declared: inheritable regions under heir custody, sealed regions encrypted under their declared terms, extinguished regions irreversibly destroyed with the destruction itself attested. The model that read the substrate forgets; the substrate does not. Persistence is of the declared record, on machines the operator or heirs control — never a hosted service holding the dead.

**Element 3 — Heir classes as receiver classes across the death boundary.** Each heir class receives a bounded view containing exactly its granted material. Views are generated such that exclusion is structural — material outside the grant is absent from the artifact, not filtered at render — so possession of a view cannot leak beyond its grant.

**Element 4 — Receipt-attested surfacing.** Every item surfaced to an heir carries a provenance receipt tracing it to the operator's verbatim record. An item without receipt cannot render as the operator's. The receipt is the sole posthumous voucher: because the operator can no longer correct the record, the record must vouch for itself, mechanically.

**Element 5 — Register-preserving interrogation under the posthumous-invention bar.** An heir may ask the inherited mind questions. Answers are assembled exclusively from the operator's recorded words — retrieved, cited, arranged — in the operator's own register, every sentence receipted. The system is structurally barred from generating content attributed to the operator beyond the record; where the record is silent the system declares the silence. The inherited mind reflects what the person declared; it never performs the person.

**Element 6 — Navigability inherited.** The heir's bounded view remains a first-person navigable space: flyable, throwable, organized by the operator's own declared relations and weights. A grandchild flies the grandparent's actual mind — its real geometry, real emphasis, real contradictions — not a memorial page and not a simulation.

**Element 7 — Custody transfer under attestation.** Keys and stewardship transfer at death per the operator's living declaration, with every transfer event, every heir access, and every view generation recorded append-only and inspectable by the heirs, so that posthumous custody is itself held to full observability by the heirs.

**Element 8 — Protected-custody mode.** For persons who cannot declare, an institution holding their record may operate the invention only in protected-custody mode: partition authority held by a declared legal proxy, all surfacing receipt-attested, the invention's constraints binding the institution. The mode exists so that the records of the disappeared and the unidentified gain the same un-buryability and anti-fabrication guarantees as a declared estate, without granting any institution the power to speak for the dead.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION AND EMBODIMENTS

In one embodiment, an operator maintains a navigable substrate of declared material spanning years. While living, the operator partitions: the work and its reasoning inheritable to the public class; family material inheritable to the family class; specified hard material sealed for twenty years; specified private regions extinguished at death. Each declaration is one act, recorded append-only, revisable until death. At death, custody transfers per the declaration. A grandchild, decades later, opens the family-class bounded view and flies the operator's actual mind: the thoughts where the operator placed them, weighted as the operator weighted them. Aiming at any thought and requesting its receipt returns the operator's verbatim words, dated and cited. Asking the mind a question returns the operator's own recorded sentences arranged in answer, each cited; asked something the operator never addressed, the mind answers that the record is silent there.

In a further embodiment, a third party attempts to insert a fabricated memory into an inherited mind. The insertion carries no provenance receipt against the operator's verbatim record and is mechanically exposed on first inspection, here doing its load-bearing work where no living operator remains to catch the fabrication.

In a further embodiment, inheritable regions containing irreversible-loss-class material keep preservation custody and anti-burial findability, such that an operator's evidence of harm done to them remains findable, intact, and attested in the heirs' view: testimony that cannot be buried by the operator's death.

In a further embodiment, the sealed disposition implements structural hold across generations: material the operator judged too hard for now but too important to extinguish unseals to its declared heir class at its declared term, the unsealing attested. In a further embodiment under protected-custody mode, an advocacy institution holds the records of unidentified persons; a declared legal proxy partitions; every surfacing to investigators or family carries receipts; and the institution is structurally unable to extend, summarize-away, or speak beyond the held record.

The elements compose; all operate on operator- or heir-controlled machines without identity-bearing hosted services; and every consequential act — partition, transfer, access, unsealing, destruction — terminates in a declared human authority and an append-only record.

5. The method of claim 1, further comprising answering an heir's interrogation of the persisted substrate exclusively by retrieval and arrangement of the operator's recorded words with per-sentence provenance attestation, the system being structurally barred from synthesizing content attributed to the operator beyond the record, and declaring silence where the record is silent.

7. The method of claim 1, further comprising transferring custody of keys and stewardship at the operator's death per the operator's living declaration, and recording every transfer, access, view generation, and unsealing event append-only.

8. The method of claim 1, wherein a sealed region unseals to its declared heir class at its declared term, the unsealing being attested, and wherein an extinguished region's destruction at death is itself attested.

A system comprising: a partition component accepting inheritability declarations only from the living operator and recording them append-only; a persistence component holding the substrate after the operator's death per the immutable partition; a view component generating per-heir-class bounded artifacts with structural exclusion of ungranted material; an attestation component requiring a provenance receipt on every posthumously surfaced item; an interrogation component constrained to retrieval and arrangement of the operator's recorded words under a structural bar against posthumous synthesis; and a custody component executing and attesting transfer at death.

10. The system of claim 9, operating in a protected-custody mode for records of persons unable to declare, wherein partition authority is held by a declared legal proxy, every surfacing carries provenance attestation, and the system's constraints bind the holding institution.

11. The system of claim 9, wherein inheritable regions containing irreversible-loss-class material inherit preservation-custody and findability protections such that the material remains findable and intact in every heir rendering.

## INDUSTRIAL APPLICABILITY AND ADVANTAGES

Every existing system forgets a person twice: once per session, and once at death. The digital-legacy industry answers the second forgetting with simulation — avatars and chat reconstructions that perform the dead by synthesis, manufacturing speech the person never spoke — while estates inherit unreadable account exports and photographs stripped of arrangement. The invention answers it structurally: the mind a person actually declared, partitioned by their own living consent, persisting under their heirs' custody, navigable in its real geometry, every item vouched by the person's own verbatim record, and silence honored where they were silent. Applications include estate and legacy systems in which a bequeathable mind passes like property but speaks only its own record; survivor and evidence custody, where testimony of harm outlives the witness un-buryably and un-fabricably; protected custody for the disappeared and unidentified, giving institutions the duty of the record without the power to speak for the dead; archival and historical deployments in which the minds of consenting public figures remain interrogable within their declared bounds; and intergenerational continuity at human scale — a grandchild flying a grandparent's actual mind, not a deepfake of it.
